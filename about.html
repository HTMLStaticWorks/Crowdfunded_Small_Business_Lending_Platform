ABOUT LENDCIRCLE

Building a More Connected Way to Fund Small Business.

We believe growing businesses deserve access to capital, and communities deserve meaningful ways to support them.

OUR STORY

Finance Should Feel More Human.

LendCircle was founded on a simple premise: the financial system should work for the people and businesses that make up our communities.

  • Connecting businesses and investors directly to remove unnecessary friction and fees.
  • Making information easier to understand through transparent data and clear reporting.
  • Supporting local economic growth by directing capital where it can have the greatest impact.
  • Creating a transparent funding experience where everyone understands the terms from day one.
