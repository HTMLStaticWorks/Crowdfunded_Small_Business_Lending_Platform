FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for funding?

You can apply for funding by clicking the "Apply for Funding" button and completing our secure online application. The process typically takes less than 15 minutes, and our team will review your application within 48 hours.

How do I create an investor account?

Click on "Start Investing" or "Sign Up" to create an investor account. You'll need to verify your identity, link a bank account, and complete a brief investor profile before you can start browsing and funding opportunities.

Where can I track my application?

Once you've submitted an application, you can log in to your Borrower Dashboard to view its status in real-time. We'll also send you email notifications at every major milestone.

How can I view my investment portfolio?

Your investment portfolio is accessible from the Investor Dashboard. There, you can view your active investments, track repayments, monitor your annualized returns, and discover new businesses to fund.

How do I contact support?

You can use the contact form on this page, email us directly at hello@lendcircle.com, or call us at +1 800 555 0148. Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 8am to 6pm PT.
